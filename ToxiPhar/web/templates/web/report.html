Interpretation

These probabilities reflect the likelihood each endpoint's production model assigns to a positive (adverse) finding, based on patterns learned from prior labeled compounds. They are intended to help prioritize which endpoints warrant closer scrutiny — not to confirm or rule out toxicity on their own.

A compound flagged here in the DANGER band should be treated as a candidate for early experimental follow-up (e.g. in vitro assay confirmation), not as a compound already disqualified. Likewise, a SAFE band result reduces priority for that endpoint but does not replace assay-based confirmation before advancing a candidate.

Model outputs are probabilistic estimates from machine learning classifiers and carry inherent uncertainty. Use alongside, not in place of, standard experimental toxicology workflows.
